Inside the paperback

More than a stack of Christmas pictures

Each page continues the story, so kids want to see what happens next.

A hidden star on every page

A little star is tucked into each picture. Kids find it, then check the answer key at the back.

Two difficulty levels

Mitten marks show which pages have big, easy shapes for toddlers and which have more detail for older kids.

Single-sided pages

Every picture has a blank back, so markers don't bleed onto the next drawing.

Color test page

Try out crayons and markers first, before starting on a favorite picture.

Draw-your-own pages

Blank pages at the back invite kids to draw their own tree and stocking.

Certificate at the end

Finish the book and become an Official Cozy Christmas Colorist.

Meet the friends of Snuggle Woods

Pip

Juniper

A floppy-eared bunny in a green pom-pom beanie.

Waddles

A little penguin who keeps his ears warm with bright red earmuffs.
