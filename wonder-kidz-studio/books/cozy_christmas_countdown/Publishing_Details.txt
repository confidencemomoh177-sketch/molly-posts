DESCRIPTION (paste into the description box)
Find a golden star every day until Christmas!

On the first day of December, Grandma Owl gives Pip the bear cub, Juniper the bunny and Waddles the penguin a little glass lantern. "Every day until Christmas, one golden star will appear somewhere in Snuggle Woods," she says. "Find them all, and on Christmas Eve the lantern will glow brighter than ever."

Cozy Christmas Countdown is a 25-day advent activity book for kids ages 4–8. Each day from December 1st to 25th has:
• A short story to read aloud together
• A picture to color, with a golden star to spot
• An activity: crafts, games, puzzles, drawing and pretend play
• A Kindness Mission to share with family and friends

Inside you'll also find a Star Tracker to color as you go, paper chain strips for a countdown chain, an answer key and a Christmas Countdown Champion certificate.

Most days take 15–30 minutes. You'll need crayons or colored pencils, a pencil, child-safe scissors (with a grown-up), glue and tape.

A cozy, screen-free Christmas tradition and a lovely early-December gift. More free printables starring Pip, Juniper and Waddles at wonderkidzstudio.pages.dev.
