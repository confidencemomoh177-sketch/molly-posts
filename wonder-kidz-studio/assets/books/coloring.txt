                        How to Use This Book
Hello, little artist! Welcome to Snuggle Woods!

Pip, Juniper and Waddles are getting ready for Christmas, and they need YOUR colors to
make it cozy.

Follow the story. Each page shows the next part of their Christmas adventure. Read the
little sentence at the bottom together.

Find the hidden star. Every page has one small star hiding somewhere. Can you spot it?
Answers are at the back of the book.

Pick your level. Pages with 1 mitten are simple, for little hands (ages 3–5). Pages with 2
mittens have more details (ages 6–8). You can color any page you like!

Test your colors first. Use the Color Test Page to try your crayons, pencils and markers.

Take your time. Color one page a day as a Christmas countdown, or color as many as you
want!

There is no wrong color. A purple bear? A rainbow penguin? Yes, please!

Tips for grown-ups:

Crayons and colored pencils work best for ages 3–5. Markers are fine; each picture has a
blank back page so colors don't bleed onto the next picture, but slip a sheet of paper behind
just in case.

Pages are single-sided, so finished pictures can be cut out and hung on the fridge or given
as Christmas cards.

Ask your child questions while they color: "What is Pip doing? What color should the cocoa
be?" It builds language and storytelling skills.

Finished the whole book? Present the Certificate at the back!

         Meet the Snuggle Woods Friends!




            Pip                       Juniper                  Waddles


Pip the bear cub loves hot cocoa, warm cookies and big hugs. He never goes
anywhere without his striped scarf.


Juniper the bunny loves making things: paper chains, ornaments and cards. Her
pom-pom hat bounces when she hops!


Waddles the penguin loves snow more than anything. Sledding, snowballs,
snowmen… he's the first one outside every morning.


Together they live in Snuggle Woods, where Christmas is the coziest time of the year.
Turn the page to begin their story!
