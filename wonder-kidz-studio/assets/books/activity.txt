                   How to Use This Book


Hello, puzzle pal! The Snuggle Woods Christmas Fair is coming, and Pip,
Juniper and Waddles need YOUR help to get ready!

Solve the puzzles. Mazes, dot-to-dots, word searches, counting, drawing and more.
Each page has a little story line at the bottom.

Look at the mittens. 1 mitten = easy, 2 mittens = a bit trickier, 3 mittens = big-kid
challenge.

Find the hidden star. There is one small star hiding on every activity page!

Use a pencil. Then you can erase and try again.

Stuck? The answers are at the back of the book. Peek only when you really need to!

Tips for grown-ups:

Ages 4–5 will enjoy the 1-mitten pages with help; ages 7–8 can do most pages
alone.

Pages are printed on both sides. Use pencils or crayons; markers may show
through.

Do a few pages a day as a holiday countdown, or bring it on trips and to
restaurants.

Pair it with Cozy Christmas, our coloring storybook with the same three friends.

     Meet the Snuggle Woods Friends




                           Pip        Juniper     Waddles




Pip the bear cub loves cocoa, cookies and big hugs. His striped scarf goes
everywhere with him.

Juniper the bunny loves making things. Her pom-pom hat bounces when she
hops!

Waddles the penguin loves snow, sleds and silly jokes. His earmuffs keep him
toasty.

This year they are helping get Snuggle Woods ready for the big Christmas Fair.
Will you help too?
