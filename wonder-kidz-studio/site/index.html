Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I get my printable after I buy it?

Checkout is handled by Gumroad. As soon as you pay, you get a download link on screen and by email, and the PDF stays in your Gumroad library so you can download it again later.

Can I print it more than once?

Yes. Buy once and print as many copies as your family needs, whether that's one favourite page or the whole book again.

What paper size are the pages?

US Letter (8.5 × 11 in). They print fine on a regular home printer.

What should kids color with?

Crayons or colored pencils work best. If you use markers, slip a sheet of paper behind the page so colors don't bleed through.

What ages are these for?

Our Cozy Christmas coloring pages are made for toddlers and kids ages 3–8, and the activity books for ages 4–8. Each product shows its age range.

Is the printable coloring book the same as the paperback?

It's the same story as our paperback, ready to print at home. The paperback is sold on Barnes & Noble if you'd rather have a printed book.

Are there free printables?

Yes! Download our free sample coloring page and this week's free page above, plus the 34-page Snuggle Woods Christmas Bundle, free on Gumroad.
